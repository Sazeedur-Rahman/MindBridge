Mental Health Resources

Build positive habits that support your mental health and academic success

National Suicide Prevention Lifeline

988 - Available 24/7 for anyone in crisis or emotional distress.

If you or someone you know is struggling, contact 988 for immediate, confidential support from trained counselors. Talk or text anytime, day or night.
Why call? Speaking with someone can help de-escalate a crisis, offer resources, and provide hope.

Guided Meditation Sessions

Relax your mind and reduce stress with science-backed meditation tracks for any mood.

Regular meditation helps calm anxiety, improves concentration, and offers a chance for meaningful reflection. Try our audio guides, from 5 to 20 minutes.
How? Find a quiet spot, follow the breathing pace, and allow thoughts to come and go freely.

Understanding Anxiety and Depression

Learn the facts, signs, and science behind the most common student mental health challenges.

Anxiety and depression affect millions. If you experience persistent sadness, worry, or loss of interest, know that help is available and recovery is possible.
Resources: Connect with campus counselors, peer support, or read stories from others who have found strategies to cope and thrive.

Stress Management Techniques

Quick exercises and habits to control stress and improve daily resilience.

Practice grounding techniques, mindful breathing, and small daily routines to keep stress in check. Learn what works best for you!
