Our Evidence-Based Approach

MindBridge leverages the latest research in digital mental health interventions, showing a medium effect size of 0.55 for depression and 0.46 for anxiety reduction. Our platform integrates:

  • Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) - Proven digital CBT modules for anxiety and depression
  • Mindfulness-Based Interventions - Guided meditation and stress reduction techniques
  • Peer Support Models - Community-driven healing and shared experiences
  • AI-Enhanced Personalization - Tailored interventions based on individual patterns
