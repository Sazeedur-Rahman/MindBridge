# MindBridge - Digital Mental Health Support System for Students

## Overview

MindBridge is a comprehensive digital mental health and psychological support platform designed specifically for students in higher education. The system combines evidence-based interventions, AI-powered support, and personalized care to address the growing mental health crisis on college campuses.

## System Features

### 🏠 Main Landing Page (index.html)

- **Contact Form**: Multi-purpose communication with categorized support options

## Evidence-Based Research Integration

The platform incorporates findings from multiple research studies:

### Digital Mental Health Intervention Effectiveness
- **Medium effect size**: 0.55 for depression, 0.46 for anxiety reduction
- **Fully automated interventions**: More effective for anxiety (0.55 vs 0.35 guided)
- **24/7 accessibility**: Reduces barriers to mental health support
- **Cost-effective**: Scalable solution for campus counseling shortages

### Student Mental Health Statistics
- **38%** of college students experience depression symptoms
- **31.9%** of teens have anxiety disorders
- **40%** of high school students report persistent sadness
- **22%** can't afford mental health services
- **20%** of adolescents have unmet mental health care needs
